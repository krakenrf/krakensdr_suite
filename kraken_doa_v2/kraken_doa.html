🔐 Digital Decoders?The voice codecs the digital decoders can use, and the installed decoder plugins. Auto detect on a plugin = it runs when a VFO's decoder is on Auto detect (untick the ones you never need: each costs CPU). Plugins are plain source folders (plugins/<id>/): to add one, copy its folder into kraken_doa_v2/plugins/, run make in kraken_doa_v2 and press ↻. To decode a VFO, tick Digital decoder on it in the Decimators box and pick Auto detect or a decoder; any number of VFOs can decode at once, each with its own tab in the panel under the waterfall. Voice plays with Demod "Digital" (or Listen in the VFO's tab). The AMBE and TETRA codecs are not shipped (licensing): you build them yourself, for personal use - whether that is allowed where you live is your responsibility.

🤖 AI Signal Lab?Points an AI coding agent (Claude Code, or another LLM CLI) at the signal in a VFO. Investigate captures it, measures it (spectrum, bursts, modulation, symbol rate, sync words) and asks the AI what it is. Create Decoder then has the AI write, build and test a decoder plugin for it, which appears in the Digital Decoders box and the VFOs' decoder lists. Custom Instructions add your own hints. The AI's answers, the conversation and its steps show in the 🤖 AI tab under the waterfall. Uses your LLM account; enable it on the Pi with python3 ai/kraken_ai.py setup.

🌐 Web Mapper?Streams DoA bearings to the KrakenSDR web mapper (built in — no separate middleware needed). KrakenPro Cloud relays to map.krakenrf.com and needs your KrakenPro API key; Local Network broadcasts the records to LAN map clients over WebSocket. Each VFO's squelch is respected: with squelch off its bearings always transmit; with squelch on, only while the squelch is open. Callsign & location come from the Station Information panel above.

📝 Local Recording?Logs DoA lobe values to a file on this device's disk (the KrakenPi), not your browser. Legacy CSV matches DOA_value.html line-for-line; SQLite is a compact, app-readable database for plotting on a map. The interval applies to both formats; below the actual DoA data rate it logs at the data rate, and duplicate frames are never written.
