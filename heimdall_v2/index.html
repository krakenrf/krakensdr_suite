Array Elements?The number of coherent channels in use. Changing it closes and reopens the RTL-SDR devices and runs a full recalibration (30-60 s). Devices beyond the count are released for other programs; choosing more channels than are attached fails and rolls back.

KrakenSDR: 5 elements. KerberosSDR (4 elements, no noise-source switch): run heimdall with --kerberos, or with --kerberos_sw when it has CKOVAL antenna switches. On Windows --kerberos_sw isn't available (it drives a Raspberry Pi's GPIO pins): heimdall runs it as --kerberos, with manual calibration.

Forward Compensation?Corrects what is in front of the KrakenSDR (cables, filters, LNAs), which the noise-source calibration cannot see. Measure each antenna chain with a VNA as a 2-port Touchstone .s2p file, upload the files and pick one per channel. The forward transmission S21 phase (and amplitude) is calibrated out relative to the reference channel at the tuned frequency, and interpolated again on every retune. The files are kept in the folder shown below, on the receiver.

Calibration?Each channel against the reference: Lag in samples (0 = aligned), the lag servo's State and how many readings in a row were at zero lag (Count), the live measured Phase and Amp (gain vs the reference; measured, not corrected), and the phase Correction applied.

Check Calibration pulses the noise source briefly, measures the residual lag and phase and recalibrates only if they drifted (a few seconds).
Full Recalibration rebuilds the lag and phase compensation from scratch (30-60 s; the DoA is invalid meanwhile).
